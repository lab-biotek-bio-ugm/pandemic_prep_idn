# Validation & Interpretation Guide

This guide explains how to interpret the results generated by the **M07 Bridge Builder** module, specifically the ranking and grading of potential diagnostic targets.

---

## 1. Analysis Outputs

The pipeline generates three primary files in the `results/[build_name]/m07_bridge/` directory:

- `bridge_report.json`: The master results file containing detailed metrics for every candidate region.
- `conserved_regions.bed`: A browser-track file for visual inspection (compatible with IGV).
- `primer_targets_manifest.yaml`: A machine-readable list of coordinates for downstream primer design tools.

---

## 2. Understanding Candidate Grades

Every candidate region is assigned a **Grade** based on its `composite_score`. This score represents a balance between physical conservation and evolutionary stability.

| Grade | Score Range | Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A** | ≥ 0.90 | **PLATINUM**: Exceptionally stable. Ideal for first-line diagnostics. |
| **B** | 0.75 - 0.89 | **GOLD**: Strong candidate with minor mutational noise. High confidence. |
| **C** | 0.60 - 0.74 | **SILVER**: Targetable but requires careful assay design to avoid low-frequency SNPs. |
| **D** | < 0.60 | **REJECT**: High mutational risk or low conservation. Avoid for diagnostic use. |

---

## 3. Interpreting Metadata Flags

The `bridge_report.json` includes several flags that provide biological context for a specific region:

- **`coding` (Boolean)**:
  - `true`: The region falls within an Open Reading Frame (ORF). These are generally more stable due to structural constraints.
  - `false`: The region is in a UTR or intergenic space.
- **`has_hyphy_data` (Boolean)**:
  - If `false`, the scoring was based *only* on conservation (Shannon Entropy). Use these results with caution as mutational hotspots may not have been detected.
- **`near_recombination` (Boolean)**:
  - If `true`, the region is close to a genomic breakpoint identified by GARD. Recombination often leads to sudden shifts in sequence identity.

---

## 4. Visualization Strategy

To visually validate a `Grade A` region:

1. Open the **Integrative Genomics Viewer (IGV)**.
2. Load the Reference Genome (defined in `config.yaml`).
3. Load the `conserved_regions.bed` file.
4. Overlap with the **Entropy Trace** (if available) to ensure the target is centered in the most stable portion of the window.

---

## 5. Decision Checklist for Primer Design

Before selecting a target from the list, verify the following:

1. [ ] **Grade is A or B**: Do not settle for Silver (Grade C) unless no other options exist.
2. [ ] **Coding Region**: Prefer regions within essential genes (e.g., MP or Polymerase) over highly variable surface genes (HA/NA).
3. [ ] **Recombination Check**: Ensure `near_recombination` is `false`.
4. [ ] **Local Validation**: Run an in-silico BLAST against the most recent local metadata once to check for extremely recent variants that might not be in the pipeline's subsample.

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Scoring Sensitivity**
> The composite score is a guide, not a final verdict. Rare variants in local outbreaks may not always be reflected in the global dataset. Always perform a final BLAST check against your specific target sequences.
